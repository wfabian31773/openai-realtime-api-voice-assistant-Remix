---
name: The after-hours on-call rotation
description: How an urgent after-hours transfer picks who to ring from v268 on (the rotation tables, the 7-day post-op surgeon route, the retry, the director levels), where every attempt is logged, and what it deliberately leaves to today's path.
---

**Built 2026-11-23 for a process that starts 2026-12-01 (v268, `src/runtime/onCall/`, operator guide `docs/ON_CALL_ROTATION.md`). Dark until `ON_CALL_ROTATION_MODE` is `test` or `live`; the migration `migrations/add_on_call_rotation.sql` is written and not applied.**

**Who is rung.** Outside weekday daytime (Mon-Fri 08:00-16:59 Pacific is not on call), an urgent after-hours transfer on the runtime's no-ivr lane rings the week's on-call provider. A week runs Tuesday 5:00 PM to the next Tuesday 8:00 AM Pacific. An override (a cover) beats the schedule, and the latest override wins. Holidays rotate like any day. On a closed-practice day the daytime hours count as on call (`holiday_daytime`). Every urgent call goes to the on-call provider, hospitals and other providers included, with no OD/MD split. The one exception is a CERTAIN identity (RULE ZERO) whose patient had surgery 0 to 7 days ago in the Console (`si_appointment_facts` joined to `si_providers`, a 1.5 s budget, no other source): that call rings the surgeon. A surgery whose surgeon is unclear, not in the pool or inactive, a Console that is slow or fails, or no confirmed identity all go to on-call, and the text says why.

**The chain.** The provider (or surgeon), then the director of that provider's region, then the final director. A region whose director is the final director (San Diego, Coachella Valley) goes straight from the provider to him, and no one is rung twice. At each level: the text with the same patient details as today (in the text, never behind a link; test texts start `[TEST MODE]`), then a call. A ring-out is logged `no_answer`, not as a failure. A second call plus a reminder text fire at the ring-out plus 60 s, and never later than 4 minutes into the level (`RETRY_GAP_MS`, `LEVEL_RETRY_DEADLINE_MS`), so both land inside 5 minutes of the original call. A key press on any call takes the patient and stops the chain. Level 0's call is the in-call warm transfer. Every later call is a callback to the provider (`/voice/on-call/ack`, `/voice/on-call/status`): it speaks no patient detail, and the provider presses a key.

**Where to look.** `on_call_attempts` holds one row per text and per call (role, provider, number, kind, outcome, the text's and the call's Twilio sids, the routing reason), keyed on the caller's CallSid. That CallSid is the conversation ID. `on_call_escalations` holds one version-checked row per call. Its patient details are nulled when the chain ends. `/voice/health` `onCall` shows the mode, the count of test phones, whether the runner is running, and `disabledBecause`. The boot line says the same.

**What it leaves alone.** The 12 urgent triggers, the triage flow, the escalation gate and the caller's words are unchanged. The text to `URGENT_NOTIFICATION_NUMBER` and the urgent ring-out ticket still go out. PCP's blind transfer, the old core and weekday daytime are unchanged too: they ring `HUMAN_AGENT_NUMBER` as before. A setting the rotation cannot honour turns all of it off rather than half-running it. That covers a mode it does not know, test mode with no `ON_CALL_TEST_PHONES`, and a caller ID equal to the Wayne Operator Line (`OPERATOR_LINE_FROM_NUMBER`). Tables that are missing or unreadable also send the call down today's path (`store_unavailable`, `rotation_unreadable`).

**Changing it.** Provider phones and emails never go in git. The first load is `scripts/on-call-seed.ts` from the two CSVs. Later changes go through `scripts/on-call-change.ts` (swap, cover, add-provider, extend, deactivate, activate, set-director). Both print SQL for the operator to read and run, written outside the repository with mode 0600. Tests use synthetic names and 555 numbers.

**Verified offline, not in production.** The migration, seed and change SQL ran on a local PostgreSQL 16. Checked there: the boundaries (Tuesday 4:59 PM, 5:00 PM, 7:59 AM, 8:00 AM Pacific), a holiday week, the San Diego, Coachella and Inland Empire chains, and the runner's walk (the retry and reminder, the director levels, a key press stopping the chain, the details nulled).
